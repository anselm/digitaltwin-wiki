# Almulhim's review of urbanization, land cover and climate

22 November 2026. Claude, in a cloud session.

## What Anselm sent

Thirteen phone screenshots of a ScienceDirect article page, reached from a
LinkedIn post by the author: "Understanding interactions among urbanization,
land use and land cover change, and climate impacts: Implications for
climate-resilient urban planning", Abdulaziz I. Almulhim. He could not fetch
the full article. He called it "an interesting whole systems way of
thinking" that "also suggests specific models", and asked for the images to
be put in order, digested, and the wiki expanded where appropriate.

## The screenshots in reading order

By the file names they arrived with: IMG_2321 (title, highlights), 2323
(highlights, abstract opening), 2322 (abstract), 2325 (abstract end,
keywords, introduction opening), 2326, 2327, 2328, 2329, 2330, 2331
(introduction), 2332 (the gap in the literature), 2333 and 2334 (what the
study does; the two overlap). A few lines in the middle of the abstract,
between "the climate" and "moderating the climatic impacts of urban
expansion", are in none of them. The screenshots are not in the repository:
they are the publisher's page behind a paywall.

## What was done

- `articles/almulhim-urbanization-land-cover-climate` (draft, tier 2): the
  loop the paper proposes, its findings from the abstract and highlights, a
  table of the measurements its introduction quotes from other studies, each
  with the work cited, and a section marked as the wiki's reading that maps
  the loop onto entries here (UrbanSim, Dynamic World, NLCD, Cool Cities
  Lab, PALM).
- `questions/land-use-change`: heat added to the list of what a change of
  cover affects, with two tools and a pointer to the entry.

## Limits

- The journal, the publication date and the DOI were not found. Two web
  searches returned other papers by the author and not this one. The entry
  has no `article.url` and its one source has no address. This needs fixing
  before promotion; the LinkedIn post or the page's address would settle it.
- This session's network blocks api.crossref.org, api.openalex.org,
  www.sciencedirect.com, www.nature.com and doi.org, so none of the cited
  works was opened. Every figure in the table is as Almulhim reports it.
- Only the abstract and introduction were read. The methods, results,
  figures and reference list were not.
- The house skills were not installed in this session (no `.claude/`
  folder), so the writing-style skill was not read. The entry follows the
  form of the fire-risk paper entry.

## Leads

- Gao and O'Neill 2020 (cited for projections of global urban land to 2100
  under socioeconomic pathways) and Winkler and others 2021 (cited for 32
  per cent of land changing use since the 1960s) each appear to rest on a
  public dataset. I guessed, from memory and not from a page read, that
  these are the SEDAC urban land projection grids and HILDA+. Each would be
  a data-source entry once the pages can be opened.
- A scenario for one growing city (the paper names Gaborone, Kigali and
  Saudi cities) would give the loop a place. Not written: one sentence in an
  introduction is not enough to write from.
- No model of the feedback from heat and flooding to later land use is on
  the wiki.
