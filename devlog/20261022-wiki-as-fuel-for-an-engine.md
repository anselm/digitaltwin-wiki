# The wiki as fuel for an engine (2026-10-22)

Anselm, after reading Google DeepMind's forest-types post (ForTy, now a
draft data source): he wondered where he could still contribute, given
how much the large labs now release, and whether a civic twin product
would only be "a shim over top of these insane capabilities".

The answer discussed: the labs ship maps of what is there now, one layer
at a time, built for global rules. Scenarios, joining layers into whole
systems for one place, turning a local worry into a question, and local
ground truth kept by the people who know are left to others. ForTy itself
is a thin layer over AlphaEarth embeddings, built by Google.

His decision, in his words: "I think I will sketch out a digital twin
product that leverages these existing data sources ... I will tackle
writing a digital twin engine / service separately next - the digital
twins wiki is fuel for that effort."

What that means here: the wiki stays an evergreen reference for others,
and it also becomes the parts catalogue for his engine. The data-source
and engine entries (access, format, licence, resolution, cadence) are the
fields such an engine needs, so keep them filled honestly. The engine
lives in its own project, not in this repo.

He also gave standing leave on 2026-10-22 to commit, push and deploy
this wiki's updates without asking each time.
