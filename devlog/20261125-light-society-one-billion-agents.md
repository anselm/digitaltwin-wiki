# Light Society: a billion simulated people

25 November 2026. Claude, in a cloud session.

Anselm sent the first page of "Modeling Earth-Scale Human-Like Societies
with One Billion Agents" (Guan and others) with no note. Taken as a paper
for the twins-of-people side, as with Maier on 21 November. Found as
arXiv:2506.12078; version 2 was read (the arXiv API gives 7 June 2025 for
version 1 and 28 June 2026 for version 2).

Written as `articles/guan-light-society-one-billion-agents` (draft, tier 2,
tagged twins of people so it shows on that shelf). No picture: the arXiv
record carries the standard distribution licence, not a Creative Commons
one.

Read: the main text, the discussion, methods 4.2 to 4.6. Not read: 2.4.4
and 4.7 on network shape, related work, figures, appendices.

What the entry is careful about. In the billion-agent run each agent has
one of 10,000 profiles, and every interaction is a lookup in a table
precomputed from a small network trained on about 400,000 language-model
decisions per topic. The entry says so in the paper's own terms and draws
the two consequences as arithmetic: 10,000 distinct people repeated about
100,000 times, and no language model running during the simulation. The
only comparison with people found in the sections read is the Ultimatum
Game offer of 41 against the 40 to 50 per cent the paper cites; the entry
says that the trust-game and opinion results are not compared with people
in what was read. The authors' own cautions are quoted.

No code address was found in the text read, though the paper speaks of
"the released framework". Worth one search before promotion.

Lead: Synthetic people (articles/synthetic-people) could take a sentence
on surrogates, the point that accuracy on single decisions did not predict
how the whole population behaved.
