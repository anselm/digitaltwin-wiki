## Neighbouring fields

A shelf in Theory, by tag `neighbouring field`, for fields readers arrive
with that sit beside modelling: Polycrisis and metacrisis, Foresight and
anticipation, Cynefin, and the Earth System Governance Project. The rule
that keeps it small: one orienting entry per field, saying what it does
and where it meets a model, not a library of that field's writing.
