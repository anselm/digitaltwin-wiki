## Mechanism

- `proposed`: my suggestion for the public seed. Treated as hidden by the
  build until he changes it.
- `public`: only he sets this.

importer now writes `hidden` by default.

public so the community can contribute, hidden entries become readable there.

## Proposed seed: one topic, water

Why water: his public modeling work is on river allocation, and water is the
one topic all three lay personas share (a farmer's irrigation, a town's
flooding, a community's river). One topic done thoroughly shows the whole
shape of the guide: question, instruments, data, method, example.

About a dozen pages:

- 1 question page, in plain words, on water: who runs short, and what floods.
- 6 instruments from the existing entries, chosen because the survey shows
  an ordinary person or small group can reach them: InVEST, Earth Index,
  En-ROADS, Geodesignhub, Deltares Delft3D, Helsinki (to be split from
  Bologna). Only InVEST and Delft3D are about water as such; the others show
  the look, use and decide-together rungs. A water-first seed needs two or
  three free water tools the survey does not list. Finding them is research
  not yet done.
- 2 data sources named in the survey: USGS Water Data, Copernicus Data Space.
- 1 concept page: what a digital twin is.
- 2 playbook stages: "Name the question", "Check it against what happened".
- 1 case: a river model of his, written from facts he supplies.

Everything else waits, hidden, and comes out as someone in the community
asks for it or offers to check it. The visible count of "not yet reviewed"
fields and a short "what should we add next" list are the public invitation.
