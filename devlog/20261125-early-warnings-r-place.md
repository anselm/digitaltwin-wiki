What the entry is careful about. Both halves of the result are given: far
better than single standard indicators, and no better than chance on 27
per cent of transitions at the 20-minute range. The list of behaviours is
introduced with the authors' own caution that the grouping involves
conjecture. The last section says the transfer to other systems is the
authors' proposal and that the paper shows the method on one system in two
years. The section headed "Safeguards worth copying" is the wiki's
selection from the paper's discussion.
