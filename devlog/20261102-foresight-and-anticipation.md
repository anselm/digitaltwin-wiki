("[wrote]" in point 2 replaces a phone autocorrect; Anselm confirmed the
slip. The book is *Anticipatory Democracy*, edited by Clement Bezold.)

## What was done

Session: Claude Code, session_01QvbEMPfgTgBCEh7BrFsFv1 (twin-compendium room).

- Book entries on the Media shelf for the foundational texts of the field, each
  read from catalogue records and publisher or library pages by an agent with
  a self-refute pass.
- One terms entry, Foresight and anticipation, that carries all three points:
  the field and where it is taught, the anticipatory-democracy history, and
  the field's deliberate vocabulary (forecast, foresight, scenarios,
  signals) against "predict".

Point 3 is not too subtle for this wiki. The site's core is engines that
predict; the futures field built its language to say it does something
else, and a reader moving between the two needs the distinction.

Claims about the field sitting "outside formal academia" are Howard's
reading; the entry states only what programmes exist and where.

Conflicts the agents found and left out rather than resolve: Mother Jones
says Gingrich started the Congressional Clearinghouse on the Future, but
the 1978 National Journal and the Wilson Center date it to 1976, before he
entered Congress. No source we could open names the "Committee for
Anticipatory Democracy" as such; the 1980 review says only that Toffler
formed a committee of over fifty in 1975. Wikipedia's dates for Cynefin's
renamings (obvious 2014, clear 2015) contradict Snowden's 2020 post, which
proposes "clear"; the entry follows Snowden.
