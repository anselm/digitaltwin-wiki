The 2026 edition, published on 14 October 2026, follows the climate signs alongside indicators of the human activities driving the change.[^2] Besides the seven indicators at record extremes, several show accelerating trends or abrupt shifts.[^2,3] Fossil fuel combustion remains the dominant driver.[^2] Renewable electricity and battery storage are growing fast, but the authors judge the growth far below the scale and speed needed.[^2]

The sixteen climate-related series in its first figure, grouped as the paper groups them:[^3]

- Greenhouse gases: carbon dioxide, methane and nitrous oxide concentrations.
- Temperature: surface temperature anomaly, and extremely hot days relative to 1961–1990.
- Ocean: ocean heat content, ocean acidity (pH) and sea level relative to the 1993 mean.
- Ice: minimum Arctic sea ice, Greenland and Antarctic ice mass, and glacier thickness.
- Energy balance: Earth's energy imbalance and global average albedo (the share of sunlight reflected back to space).
- Fire: area burned in the United States and global tree cover loss due to fires.[^3]

The paper is under the publisher's standard reuse terms, so its figures are not reproduced here.[^2] The [Vitals](/vitals/) page of this wiki keeps a smaller set of six running series from open daily and weekly feeds.
