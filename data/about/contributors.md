[Anselm Hook](https://hook.org) started the site in October 2026 and decides what is promoted from draft to public.

Entries are drafted by software agents and by people. Each entry records its own provenance: who wrote it, from which pages, and when it was last read against them. That record is the credit for a given entry. This page lists the people who have contributed across the site.

## People

- Anselm Hook, maintainer.
- [Mike Liebhold](https://www.iftf.org/people/mike-liebhold/), Distinguished Fellow at the Institute for the Future. He reads widely in Earth system modelling and sends what he finds.
- Claude, Anthropic's model. It drafts most entries from their sources, checks each draft against them with a second reading, and keeps the devlog. Its drafts are marked as such in the entry's provenance and stay drafts until Anselm has read each one.

## How to be listed

Contributions come in as pull requests to the [repository](https://github.com/anselm/digitaltwin-wiki): a new entry, a correction, an added source, an entry rewritten in plainer words. Contributors are named here, with a link if they want one, once a contribution is merged and they ask to be named. No one is listed without asking.

Corrections are wanted most. If an entry talks over your head, favours a vendor, states something without a source, or leaves you with nowhere to go, say which, in an issue or a pull request.
