The site is maintained by [Anselm Hook](https://hook.org), who started it in October 2026 and decides what is promoted from draft to public.

Entries are drafted by software agents and by people. Each entry carries its own provenance: who wrote it, from which pages, and when it was last read against them. That record, not this page, is the credit for a given entry; this page lists the people who have contributed across the site.

## People

- Anselm Hook, maintainer.

## How to be listed

Contributions come in as pull requests to the [repository](https://github.com/anselm/digitaltwin-wiki): a new entry, a correction, a source added, an entry rewritten in plainer words. A contributor is listed here by name, with a link if they want one, once a contribution is merged and they ask to be named. No one is listed without asking.

Corrections are the most valuable contribution. If an entry talks over your head, favours a vendor, states something without a source, or leaves you with nowhere to go, say which one, in an issue or a pull request.
