This page is the wiki's working list: where it stands, page by page, and what to do next. It is rebuilt with the site.

Each entry gets a rough maturity score out of 100. It adds up whether a person wrote or has read it and whether it has been promoted, whether it lists its sources, its length, a description that fits a search result, a picture, how few machine-prose tells it has, and whether other pages link to it. The score is a way to sort, not a grade.

The open items are the project's own list, `todo.md`, shown as it stands in the repository.
