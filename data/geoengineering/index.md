Nobody can try a climate intervention on the planet first, so nearly everything known about one comes from a model. That makes this the part of the wiki where the model carries the most weight, and where what a model leaves out matters most.

The entries here fall on two branches. One reflects sunlight, with aerosol in the stratosphere or with brightened marine cloud. The other takes carbon dioxide back out of the air, on land or in the ocean. [Geoengineering](/terms/geoengineering/) defines the methods and follows the modelling from a single spray plume to the globe. [Cooling the planet](/questions/cooling-the-planet/) starts from the question a person in one region would ask: what would this do to the weather where I live?

The shelf gathers entries from across the wiki by one tag and moves none of them. The data sources are the shared experiments that Earth system models have run, and their output is free to download. The articles are single studies, each with what it assumed and what it could not show.
