A use case is a kind of question, such as where a flood will go. A challenge is one real instance, in one place, with people waiting on the answer. Most of them cross several domains at once: an oil spill runs from ocean currents to food webs to fisheries to the towns that live on them. Each page says what happened, who needs to decide something, which domains the problem chains together, what a model would let them try, and what data and tools exist to start.
