A model of a place is only as good as what it has been checked against. The entries here are findings that something in the world has changed, by a stated amount, against a stated baseline: rivers that fell, forests that died back, a sea that emptied. They are kept for what a model can do with them, which is one of three things.

- **A target.** The finding gives a number a model should be able to reproduce, such as the size of a decline over a period.
- **A relationship.** The finding ties one quantity to another, such as warm seas to failed rains, and a model of the place has to carry that link.
- **A threshold.** The finding places a limit beyond which a system behaves differently.

A finding with none of these is news, and the [News](/posts/) shelf lists it by date. The measurements that are taken continuously are on [Vitals](/vitals/), which redraws nine global series at every build. Problems in one named place that a model could take on are [Scenarios](/scenarios/). The page on [tipping points](/terms/tipping-points/) covers thresholds as a subject.

The shelf gathers entries by one tag and moves none of them. Each says what was measured, with what data, and what was not read.
