En-ROADS stands for Energy Rapid Overview and Decision Support.[^2] Moving sliders for energy sources, carbon pricing, land use, economic growth and other levers shows at once what happens to global emissions, temperature, energy use and related indicators.[^2,3]

Climate Interactive aims it at policymakers, business and finance, advocates and educators.[^1] Trained ambassadors run facilitated group sessions with it: a Climate Solutions Workshop and a Climate Action Simulation Game.[^1] Its users include the head of the City of Stuttgart's climate protection office, quoted on the site.[^1]

Under the interface is an integrated assessment model, a model that links the economy, energy and climate.[^2] It is built with system dynamics, a method of modelling stocks, flows and feedback loops.[^2] Eighteen linked submodels run from 1990 to 2100, using behavioural decision rules and feedback loops instead of optimisation with perfect foresight.[^2] Starting values come from historical data such as UN population figures and measured greenhouse-gas concentrations.[^2] Outputs are compared with other integrated assessment models, and a technical reference lists the equations and sources.[^2] The interface is offered in more than 20 languages.[^1,2]

Updates in July 2026 added ocean alkalinity enhancement (adding alkaline minerals to seawater so it absorbs more carbon dioxide) and the energy demand of data centres and AI.[^1]
