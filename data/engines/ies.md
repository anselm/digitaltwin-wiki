The digital twin work of IES (Integrated Environmental Solutions Limited) combines simulation with measured data from building management systems, utility bills, IoT (internet-connected) sensors and meters.[^1] One model can cover a building, campus, portfolio or city.[^1] It is used for questions of energy, carbon, cost and comfort through design, retrofit, commissioning and operation.[^1]

IES lists six cases.[^1] A live twin of Pollok Country Park in Glasgow maps where energy is used and generated across the estate.[^1] For Dublin City Council, three social housing blocks were modelled to compare the whole-life carbon of four retrofit strategies against 2030 and 2050 targets.[^1] Another model covers the whole city of Bertam in Penang.[^1]

The software is commercial. The tools work together or alone:[^1]

- VE, for design and retrofit analysis
- IES Live, for operational energy and carbon
- iCD, for energy analysis across portfolios, campuses and cities, also as a SketchUp plugin
- iSCAN, for in-use building data
- iVN, for district heating, local energy networks and renewables
- Sync, for Revit[^1]

The current VE release is labelled 2026.[^1] There is a free trial and a demo, and prices are not published.[^1] IES also sells consultancy on the same work.[^1]
