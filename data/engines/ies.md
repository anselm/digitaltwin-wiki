The digital twin work of IES (Integrated Environmental Solutions Limited) combines simulation with measured data from building management systems, utility bills, IoT (internet-connected) sensors and meters. One model can cover a building, campus, portfolio or city. It is used for questions of energy, carbon, cost and comfort through design, retrofit, commissioning and operation.

IES lists eight cases. A live twin of Pollok Country Park in Glasgow maps where energy is used and generated across the estate. For Dublin City Council, three social housing blocks were modelled to compare the whole-life carbon of four retrofit strategies against 2030 and 2050 targets. Another model covers the whole city of Bertam in Penang.

The software is commercial. The tools work together or alone:

- VE, for design and retrofit analysis
- IES Live, for operational energy and carbon
- iCD, for energy analysis across portfolios, campuses and cities, also as a SketchUp plugin
- iSCAN, for in-use building data
- iVN, for district heating, local energy networks and renewables
- Sync, for Revit

The current VE release is labelled 2026. There is a free trial and a demo, and prices are not published. IES also sells consultancy on the same work.
