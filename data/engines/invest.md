InVEST has more than 20 free models for mapping and valuing the goods and services people get from nature.[^1] They are meant to inform decisions about land and water.[^1] Examples of use are in the Alliance's featured projects and publications.[^1]

The models run in the InVEST Workbench, a downloadable application with a user guide and data sources.[^1] Community plugins extend the Workbench through a plugin registry.[^1] A Data Hub offers global layers and pre-processed inputs.[^1] Support comes through a community forum, video tutorials and a self-paced online course, and bugs and contributions go through public issue trackers.[^1] The Alliance (NatCap) publishes a quarterly software column.[^1]
