Awana Digital is a San Francisco non-profit that builds decentralised technology with Indigenous partners.[^2] It also makes Terrastories and the Earth Defenders Toolkit.[^2] Awana says fourteen Indigenous peoples across the Amazon, East Africa, Southeast Asia and the Pacific co-designed CoMapeo, including the Achuar, the Maasai and the Ogiek.[^1]

A team sets up a project and records observations against a shared set of categories, either its own or a curated set from the Earth Defenders Toolkit library.[^1] Devices exchange data peer to peer; an optional server can archive a project's data for teams that cannot meet in person.[^1,3] Awana reports about 800 land defenders using its tools across 75 projects in 38 countries, and more than 7 million hectares mapped.[^2]

CoMapeo is free and open source.[^1,2] It is distributed for Android as an APK (an installable app file) and through the Google Play Store.[^1] Awana invites feedback and publishes release notes.[^1]
