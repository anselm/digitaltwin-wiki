Technosylva, Inc. was formed in 1997 and has offices in La Jolla, California and León, Spain.[^1,2] Its models draw on climate, vegetation, topography and human activity.[^2] Besides utilities and fire agencies, it sells to insurance and credit agencies.[^1]

The products split by task:

- FireSight: asset-level wildfire risk for planning and investment.[^1]
- FireRisk: operational risk forecasts for decisions such as public safety power shutoffs.[^1]
- Tactical Analyst: one situational-awareness view combining data sources, for predictive analysis and fire management.[^1]
- fiResponse: incident management for emergency response across hazards.[^1]
- An extreme-weather product: forecasts of grid impacts.[^1]
- A flood-planning product: flood exposure of assets.[^1]

Customers shown on its site include CAL FIRE, Pacific Gas & Electric and San Diego Gas & Electric.[^1] It says its products are in operational use in nine countries across North America, Europe and South America.[^2]

Technosylva has a joint venture with the US Forest Service on wildfire science.[^2] The company says its models are validated with fire agencies in the field.[^2] It is commercial software, and pricing is not published.[^1,2]
