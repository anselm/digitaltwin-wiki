Aimsun, based in Barcelona, sells software and services for transport planning and operations. Aimsun Next is for impact studies. Aimsun Live forecasts traffic in the near term, so a traffic control centre can act before congestion builds.

Aimsun lists three deployments. For the Florida Department of Transportation it ranks candidate response plans for regional highway congestion. For National Highways in Oxfordshire it forecasts traffic and nitrogen dioxide up to an hour ahead. For Tees Valley Combined Authority it runs the Tees Valley Traffic Digital Twin. The twin combines signal data, roadside data and the Bus Open Data Service to spot late-running buses and trigger pre-approved signal timing plans. The authority says it is running at the Urban Traffic Management and Control centre in Middlesbrough, initially on ten bus routes, and will be evaluated over a year.

Aimsun Next comes in editions with published prices and a free trial. The current release is Aimsun Next 26 (2026). Aimsun also sells traffic data.
