Aimsun, based in Barcelona, sells software and services for transport planning and operations.[^2,4] Aimsun Next is for impact studies.[^2] Aimsun Live forecasts traffic in the near term, so a traffic control centre can act before congestion builds.[^1]

Aimsun lists three deployments.[^1] For the Florida Department of Transportation it ranks candidate response plans for regional highway congestion.[^1] For National Highways in Oxfordshire it forecasts traffic and nitrogen dioxide up to an hour ahead.[^1] For Tees Valley Combined Authority it runs the Tees Valley Traffic Digital Twin.[^1,3] The twin combines signal data, roadside data and the Bus Open Data Service to spot late-running buses and trigger pre-approved signal timing plans.[^1,3] The authority says it is running at the Urban Traffic Management and Control centre in Middlesbrough, initially on ten bus routes, and will be evaluated over a year.[^3]

Aimsun Next comes in editions with published prices and a free trial.[^1,2] The current release is Aimsun Next 26 (2026).[^1,2] Aimsun also sells traffic data.[^2]
