The third layer of the stack: the simulations that move a place forward in time. They are shelved by standing, from the few that a practitioner in the field would name first, through specialist and newer tools, to research codes and projects that have gone quiet or cannot be had. The ranking rests on each tool's own record: recent releases, citations, named users, and whether an outsider can get it. Each entry says who makes it, what it is used for, how it runs and what is open. The data they eat is on the [Data](/data-sources/) shelf, the things they reason about on [Ontologies](/ontologies/), and the viewers that show their results on [Presentation](/presentation/). [Capture](/capture/) and [Platforms](/platforms/) have shelves of their own.
