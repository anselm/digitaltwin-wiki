The third layer of the stack: the simulations and platforms that move a place forward in time, shelved by the task a person is trying to do. Each entry says who makes it, what it is used for, how it runs and what is open. The data they eat is on the [Data](/data-sources/) shelf, the things they reason about on [Ontologies](/ontologies/), and the viewers that show their results on [Presentation](/presentation/).
