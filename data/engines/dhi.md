DHI makes water modelling software (the MIKE family) and runs water projects; it has worked on water environments for over fifty years. DHI CityFlow Live is its software for operating an urban water system in real time, with modules for sewer networks, treatment plants and receiving waters. This entry covers the Treatment Plant module, previously sold as TwinPlant.

The module runs a process model of a wastewater treatment plant against the plant's live data. Operators see current status and forecast performance over the next one to two days from weather and predicted influent. They can test operating and maintenance strategies on the model instead of on the plant. The Optimised Plant sub-module tunes aeration controllers automatically. DHI's named case is Gruppo CAP. There the model was coupled to SCADA data (the readings from the plant's control system) at the Bresso-Niguarda plant, to show how parameter changes affect effluent, sludge, energy use and biogas.

It is commercial. DHI installs it with the utility after evaluating the plant. Pricing is not published.
