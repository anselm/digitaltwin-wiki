DHI makes water modelling software (the MIKE family) and runs water projects; it has worked on water environments for over fifty years.[^2] DHI CityFlow Live is its software for operating an urban water system in real time, with modules for sewer networks, treatment plants and receiving waters.[^1] This entry covers the Treatment Plant module, previously sold as TwinPlant.[^1]

The module runs a process model of a wastewater treatment plant against the plant's live data. Operators see current status and forecast performance over the next one to two days from weather and predicted influent.[^1] They can test operating and maintenance strategies on the model instead of on the plant.[^1] The Optimised Plant sub-module tunes aeration controllers automatically.[^1] DHI's named case is Gruppo CAP.[^1] There the model was coupled to SCADA data (the readings from the plant's control system) at the Bresso-Niguarda plant, to show how parameter changes affect effluent, sludge, energy use and biogas.[^1,3]

It is commercial. DHI installs it with the utility after evaluating the plant.[^1] Pricing is not published.
