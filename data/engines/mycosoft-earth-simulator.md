Mycosoft is a US company (Mycosoft, Inc., with an operating subsidiary, Mycosoft, LLC).[^1,2] It builds fungal computer interfaces, field sensing devices and environmental software.[^2] On the Earth Simulator globe a user can turn on environmental layers, check where the company's devices are and what state they are in, play back past conditions, view forecasts and run simulated scenarios.[^1] The globe is one part of NatureOS, the company's platform for sensing, simulation and environmental records.[^1]

Mycosoft aims the simulator at planners, scientists and analysts as a model of the world across space and time.[^1,2] Its data comes from two sources. One is Mycosoft's own field hardware: sensing droids for ground, air and water, linked by a mesh network the company calls DirtNet.[^1,2] The other is MINDEX, the company's database of species, observation and compound records, each stored with its origin, time and location.[^3]

The simulator runs as a hosted web application.[^1] There is a plans and access page, but no prices or licence are published.[^2] Mycosoft's about page notes that product information does not by itself establish live data or scientific validation.[^2] Mycosoft says it has been building since 2021.[^1,2,3]
