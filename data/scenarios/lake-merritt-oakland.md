Lake Merritt is a brackish tidal lagoon in central Oakland, 8 to 10 feet deep.[^10] The Lake Merritt Institute gives it 140 acres and the Water Board 150.[^6,10] A 4,650-acre urban watershed drains into it through more than 60 storm drains, and Bay water reaches it through the Lake Merritt Channel.[^5,9,10] Oakland's Measure DD bond, approved in 2002, paid in part for opening that channel.[^14] In July 2013 a dam and a twelve-lane road at 12th Street were replaced with bridges, a boulevard and a park, which SPUR reported raised the lake's tidal flow by 50 percent.[^12,14] A second Measure DD project, to replace the culverts under 10th Street with a bridge, was under way in 2015.[^13,14] Tide gates at the county's pump station near the channel mouth still control how much Bay water comes in, and are closed for flood control and other needs.[^8,9]

In August 2022 a bloom of the alga *Heterosigma akashiwo* spread from the Bay into the lake.[^3,6] Oxygen fell to near zero across most of it for several days,[^6] and fish died in large numbers: the City says tens of thousands,[^3] and a later Water Board report puts the toll at up to 10,000 organisms.[^8] The lake is listed as impaired for low dissolved oxygen, and the Water Board is now writing a restoration plan for it.[^6]

## Who needs an answer

- The City of Oakland, which has to choose which measures to install and where. It ran a pilot of aeration and oxygenation devices from 2023 and replaced the one that did not work with a diffused aeration system in January 2026.[^2,11] It has $959,757 in federal money for a Healthy Lake Initiative running to 2029.[^4,11]
- The San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board, whose Advance Restoration Plan will recommend actions for a City lake management plan.[^6]
- The Alameda County Flood Control and Water Conservation District, which operates the tide gates[^9] and has to weigh flood protection against flushing.
- Rowers and sailors, and the volunteers of the Lake Merritt Institute and the Rotary Nature Center Friends, who reported the two small die-offs of June 2025.[^6,8]

## The domains it chains

Tides and gate operation in the channel.[^9] Rain and storm drains, which can lay fresh water over the salt.[^5,7] Nutrients, more than 99% of which the Water Board estimates come in from the Bay, and the algae they feed.[^9] Oxygen at the surface and on the bottom, which differ most in the narrow Glen Echo arm, while both arms fall short of oxygen more often than the center.[^7] Shrimp, gobies and larger fish, and the people who row, sail and walk around the lake every day.

## What a twin should let people try

- Gates: how long a closure can last in summer or winter before the bottom water of the arms runs out of oxygen. The first June 2025 die-off came two days after the gates reopened from an eleven-day closure, the second after a shorter closure of about a day. The Water Board could not tie them to gate operation and plans a fuller analysis of past closures.[^8]
- Flushing against feeding: more Bay water mixes and oxygenates the lake but also brings in the nitrogen that limits algae there.[^9] What does more or less exchange do to both?
- Aeration: where fountains or diffusers would do the most, given that the oxygenation device in the Glen Echo arm did not perform as expected.[^2]
- The next Bay bloom: how fast a red tide like 2022's would empty the lake of oxygen, and whether early gate closure or aeration would buy time.

## Where to start

The City and the Lake Merritt Institute have run three continuous monitoring stations since 2023, measuring oxygen, temperature and salinity at surface and bottom.[^7] Their readings are published through the City's LakeTech dashboard.[^6] The Water Board ran two more from June to October 2024 and posts its nutrient data as a spreadsheet beside its reports.[^6,7,9] Dead fish have been logged on [iNaturalist](/data-sources/inaturalist/), where the California Department of Fish and Wildlife set up an SF Bay Harmful Algae Bloom 2023 project for such reports.[^3] A coastal flow and water-quality model such as [Delft3D](/engines/deltares-delft3d/) could take the channel, the gates and the lake together.
