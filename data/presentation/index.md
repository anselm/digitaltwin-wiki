The last layer of the stack: the libraries and applications that draw a model's output as a globe, a map, a scene or a chart in a browser or on a desktop. They do not simulate anything; they make a result visible, explorable and comparable, which is where most people meet a model. Several run entirely in a browser with no account, which matters for a classroom or a public meeting.
