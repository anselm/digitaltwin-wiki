The hub is a membership portal for professionals. Connected Places Catapult notes on the site that what members post does not necessarily reflect its views. The hub guides data sharing and standards for connected digital twins. New members are asked to start with the Gemini Principles before developing a twin, then to build skills through the hub's resources and online learning.

The learning material, the resources and the community discussions all need an account. The news feed, the events listing and the hub's "Gemini Call" sessions on YouTube are public. Membership cost is not published.
