The Digital Twin Hub is a membership portal for professionals working on connected digital twins, run by Connected Places Catapult, which says on the site that the content posted by members does not necessarily reflect its views. The home page describes the site as guiding data sharing and standards for connected digital twins and invites new members to start with the Gemini Principles before developing a twin, then to build skills through its resources and online learning.

Most of the site is behind a sign-up: the learning material, the resources and the community discussions. What is public is the news feed and the events listing, and the hub's "Gemini Call" sessions are published on YouTube; both are polled as sources for this wiki. The pages read do not say what membership costs or who may join.
