Many people arrive at a model because something binds them: a treaty target, a national law, a council motion, a court order. Each entry here says who issued the mandate, whom it binds, what it requires by when, how progress against it is counted, and which [use case](/questions/) it turns into. A mandate is a reason to model, not a model; the engines and data sit one link away.
