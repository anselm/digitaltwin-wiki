Mike Liebhold is a Distinguished Fellow and senior researcher in the Technology Futures Lab at the Institute for the Future.[^1] His work there covers whole systems, data networks, immersive media and spatial and contextual computing, with clients, researchers and public bodies including HHS, DARPA, the FCC, Congress and the White House.[^1] Before IFTF he was a senior researcher at Atari, Apple, Netscape and Intel.[^1]

For this wiki he reads widely in Earth system modelling and sends what he finds.
