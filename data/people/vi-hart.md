Vi Hart, born in 1988, is an American mathematician and online video creator who describes themself as a "recreational mathemusician".[^1] Their videos, starting in 2010 with a series on doodling in maths class, made them widely known, and they made videos for Khan Academy.[^1] With Matt Parker they won the 2018 Communications Award of the Joint Policy Board for Mathematics.[^1] They have co-authored papers on computational geometry and the mathematics of paper folding.[^1]

In 2014 Hart co-founded eleVR, a group researching virtual reality.[^1] The same year Hart and Nicky Case made the playable post Parable of the Polygons on Thomas Schelling's model of segregation.[^1] In 2016 eleVR joined the Human Advancement Research Community at Y Combinator Research, where Hart was a principal investigator.[^1] Hart later worked in the Societal Resilience Group at Microsoft, as a senior research project manager and a director of policy and strategy, and left Microsoft and the technology industry in 2022.[^1]
