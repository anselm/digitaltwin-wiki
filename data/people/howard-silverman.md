Howard Silverman writes and teaches about systems, design and futures, and is with the MA in Sustainable Design program at Minneapolis College of Art and Design.[^1] He developed curriculum for systems and futures courses in the Collaborative Design MFA at Pacific Northwest College of Art and taught for several years in the ITP program at NYU.[^1] From 1999 to 2012 he worked at Ecotrust in Portland, Oregon, starting as webmaster and ending as senior writer and analyst.[^1] He is a trustee of the American Society for Cybernetics and a past board member of the International Society for the Systems Sciences.[^1]

His blog, [Solving for Pattern](https://www.solvingforpattern.org/), is about theories of change, practices of engagement and ways of learning.[^1]
