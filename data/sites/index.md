"Digital twin" is shorthand. The subject is the whole family of models and
simulations of places and the systems that run through them: city and
transport models, agent-based and system-dynamics models, earth-system,
flood and fire models, decision support built on any of them. The searches
below look for all of those phrases, not the one term; the list is the
`vocabulary` in this page's own markdown, and adding a phrase there widens
every search at once. General feeds such as GOV.UK or OGC are filtered by the
same list; a feed that is about the subject already is taken whole.
