The first layer of the stack: what was measured or recorded about a place, and on what terms it can be had. Each entry says what a source covers, at what resolution and cadence, how to get it and what it costs. What a model makes of the data is the next layer, [Ontologies](/ontologies/); how the layers fit is in [The parts of a digital twin](/articles/parts-of-a-twin/).
