GRIIDC, the Gulf of Mexico Research Initiative Information and Data Cooperative, keeps the data from research on the Gulf's coasts and open water. It sits at the Harte Research Institute of Texas A&M University-Corpus Christi, and the old Gulf Research Initiative address now leads to it. Searching and downloading are free. Groups that want to store data there pay a fee.

Each dataset has its own DOI, which makes it citable down to a single cruise. The 2026 [study of the Gulf's midwater collapse](/articles/immense-but-not-immutable-deep-pelagic-decline/) lists 27 of them: raw echosounder recordings, the logs of the net's sensors, species counts with weights and measurements for each cruise from 2011 to 2023, and the genetic sequences. Anyone building a model of the Gulf after [an oil spill](/questions/after-an-oil-spill/) can test it against those records.
