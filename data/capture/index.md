Tools that read what a camera or a scanner recorded of a place and name what they find. Detectors find animals in camera-trap pictures and fish in reef video. Classifiers mark flooded ground or damaged buildings in satellite scenes. Reconstruction models build point clouds and surfaces from a phone video or a drone flight. Many are open models a person can run on their own pictures; each entry says what goes in and what comes out.
