The layer under Data. The tools here read what a camera or a scanner recorded of a place and name what they find. Detectors find animals in camera-trap pictures and fish in reef video. Classifiers mark flooded ground or damaged buildings in satellite scenes. Reconstruction models build point clouds and surfaces from a phone video or a drone flight. What they produce is data like that on the [Data](/data-sources/) shelf, and it feeds the [Engines](/engines/) the same way. Many are open models a person can run on their own pictures; each entry says what goes in and what comes out.
