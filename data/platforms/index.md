Where models and data live and run. A geographic information system holds the layers of a place. A cloud twin service links them to live sensor data. An imagery platform runs analysis next to a satellite archive, and a research environment lets a team share notebooks and workflows. Routing and index services sit here too: they compute a route or a drought index on request rather than simulate a place. None of these is a simulation engine, but the [Engines](/engines/) often run on them, the [Data](/data-sources/) is often stored in them, and results go out through [Presentation](/presentation/).
