Where models and data are kept and run. A geographic information system holds the layers of a place. A cloud twin service links them to live sensor data. An imagery platform runs analysis next to a satellite archive, and a research environment lets a team share notebooks and workflows. Routing and index services compute a route or a drought index on request.
