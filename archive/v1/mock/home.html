A public guide to digital twins

Model Your Place

Tools for seeing how a farm, a town, a watershed or a region behaves, and what would change if you did something differently. What you can use today, what it costs, and how others have done it.

Who this is for, and what it is trying to do

For people who want to understand a place they care about: someone who farms or manages land, someone at a town or agency, a group of neighbours with a question, and the people who build these models. The aim is to help you see your own place better and leave with a next step, including when the answer is that you do not need a model.

The full statement lives in the guide's repository as corpus/about/audience-and-goals.md, and every page can be checked against it.

What do you want to see?

  • Traffic and public transportWhere will vehicles go after a closure, and can signal changes help a late bus? Aimsun · SUMO · MATSim · Tees Valley's traffic twin
  • Crowds and evacuationHow will people move through a station or leave an area during an emergency? AnyLogic · GAMA
  • Electricity and energy transitionsWhere will electrification require more network capacity, storage or generation? Neara · PyPSA · Isle of Wight energy demonstrator
  • Flooding, rivers and water servicesWhich areas will flood, and how will changes to rivers, drainage or pumps affect them? Deltares Delft3D · DHI · Xylem Vue
  • Wildfire and exposureWhere might a fire spread, which assets are exposed, and where would mitigation help? Technosylva
  • Housing and land useHow might zoning or transport investment change development and household location? UrbanSim · Urbanly CityCompass
  • Ecosystems and land managementHow might land-use changes affect water, habitat, carbon or agricultural production? InVEST · DSSAT · GCAM · Earth Index
  • Weather and climateWhat conditions are likely next week, and how might a region's climate change over decades? WeatherNext · NVIDIA Earth-2 · Destination Earth
  • Sensory landscapeHow do sound, light, smell and barriers affect people and other animals? Walk Score · CadnaA · Radiance · GRAL · Circuitscape
  • Public discussion and learningWhat changes when we combine different climate policies or land-use proposals? En-ROADS · Geodesignhub

If you do build one

Nine stages, each with a checklist, what you can do yourselves, and when to bring in help.

  1. Name the question
  2. Talk to the people affected
  3. Find the data
  4. Choose the kind of model
  5. Build the smallest version
  6. Check it against what happened
  7. Protect people's privacy
  8. Put it in front of its users
  9. Keep it alive

Design noteThe stages are a proposal and have not been checked against how real projects ran. Each opens a page with a small flow diagram and a checklist. The same checklists are packaged for an AI assistant to walk a group through.
