# Novel bamboo uses, candidates for their own entries (2026-10-23)

Found while writing the bamboo scenario (data/scenarios/bamboo-as-a-land-use-philippines.md),
after Anselm said new uses of bamboo are "changing the value landscape even more".
Each link was opened by the writer agent unless marked otherwise. Not yet entries.

- Structural round bamboo, ISO 22156:2021: https://committee.iso.org/standard/73831.html
- Glued laminated bamboo, ISO 7567:2024, the first standard for an engineered bamboo product: https://www.inbar.int/defining-the-future-of-structural-glued-laminated-bamboo/
- Engineered bamboo lumber and panels, Rizome's plant in Cagayan de Oro: https://context.ph/2023/05/30/engineered-lumber-from-bamboo-coming-out-of-a-p5-5-billion-facility-in-cdo/
- Bamboo as a Substitute for Plastic (INBAR, launched Nov 2022; action plan Nov 2023): https://www.inbar.int/basp/ and https://www.inbar.int/bamboo-as-a-substitute-for-plastics-in-the-built-environment/
- Textile fibre from endemic Philippine species: https://ispweb.pcaarrd.dost.gov.ph/sustainable-and-eco-responsive-bamboo-textile-fiber-processing/
- Bamboo charcoal, briquettes and pyroligneous vinegar (FPRDI kiln): https://www.dost.gov.ph/nowledge-resource/news/48-2017-news/1289-dost-s-bambooo-charcoal-technology
- Bamboo carbon credits: the Rizome Kawayan VM0047 project https://www.climateimpact.com/news-insights/news/climate-impact-partners-and-rizome-register-asias-first-vm0047-project-with-verra/ ; INBAR on carbon markets https://www.inbar.int/integrating-bamboo-into-carbon-markets/ ; Gold Standard https://www.inbar.int/bamboo-included-in-new-gold-standard-ar-methodology/
- Bamboo artificial reefs, Southern Leyte (seen only as a teaser on https://www.inbar.int/country/philippines/, not opened)
