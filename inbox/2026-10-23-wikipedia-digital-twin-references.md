Key to "opened":
- **read**: the page or PDF itself was read.
- **record+abs**: publisher's abstract read via its Crossref record.
- **record**: Crossref record read, no abstract in it (title, venue, date only).
- **blocked**: the site refused the request (403 or bot check).
- **dead**: 404, no response, or the domain now serves something else.
