## Noumenal: solar grazing robot (post dated Oct 21)

- A simulation of a real 14-acre solar site, "developed with expert solar grazers": sheep rotate through 10 paddocks while a robot called Whiplash trims what they leave, including areas the sheep cannot reach, to meet a 12-inch vegetation clearance through 30 days of peak-summer growth.
- The post says the U.S. has "million-plus acres of solar farms" that must meet vegetation codes (their figure; verify).
- Hard parts named: localisation without good GPS among identical rows of panels; glare, shade, dust and vegetation that changes week to week; navigation with no roads, curbs or traffic rules and livestock in the way.
- Approach: active inference, "knowing when to act and when to gather more information first", citing MOTOR Ai's talk at the IWAI conference on "epistemic AI" for Level 4 driving.
- Names tagged: Beau Carter, Jason Fox, Patrick Huembeli.
- Possible use: an agrivoltaics / solar grazing scenario (land shared by panels, sheep and machines), or an engine entry if Noumenal publishes the simulation.

## Arturo Montieri: DEA Next on the DestinE Platform (post about 18h old)

- Presented at the Agentic AI for Earth Observation workshop, hosted by BIFOLD (Berlin Institute for the Foundations of Learning and Data) and ESA Φ-lab: "DEA Next: Making Earth Intelligence Conversational through Agentic AI".
- In one live conversation the agent: discovered its connected services; showed Destination Earth layers on a 3D globe; put the CAMS dust forecast on a story slide; found the EMSR929 wildfire in Greece (Copernicus EMS) and mapped its area; mapped population density and charted unemployment from Eurostat; computed Sentinel-2 NDVI over Sicily on the Copernicus Data Space "with code you can check, edit and rerun"; built climate charts from Destination Earth's Earth Data Hub; turned an uploaded CSV into a dashboard; saved every step as an interactive story in the DEA Story Viewer.
- Destination Earth is EU-funded, implemented by ESA, ECMWF and EUMETSAT.
- Possible use: the wiki has no Destination Earth entry yet (only mentions); DestinE itself belongs on Notable twins, with DEA as its storytelling/presentation layer. Relevant to the "what is it for" thread: natural language as the way a non-technical person asks a model a question.
